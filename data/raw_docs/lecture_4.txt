# When to use a stacked chart?

Stacked chart is about composition. It is used when you want to show how a total amount is divided into different sub-groups or segments.

---

# When to use a stacked chart?
| Rule / Condition for Use | Unique Keywords | Audience Question Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total/Internal Segments Relationship: Use when the primary goal is to show the total magnitude of a category while simultaneously showing the internal segments. | Composition, Breakdown, Contribution, Mix, Ratio, Proportion, Cumulative, Sum, Sub-division, Segment | "What is the total budget for the year, and how much of it is contributed by each department?" |
|  |  | "Which region has the highest sales, and what is the product mix (Tablets vs. Laptops) in that region?" |
|  |  | "How is our total workforce broken down by seniority level across all global branches?" |

---

# When to use a clustered chart?

Clustered chart is about side-by-side comparison. In a clustered chart, sub-categories sit side-by-side on the same baseline. This makes it much easier for the audience to compare the individual heights of the "segments" across different categories, though it becomes harder to see the total sum.

---

# When to use a clustered chart?
| Rule / Condition for Use | Unique Keywords | Audience Question Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grouped Evaluation: Use when you want to show sub-categories together without combining them into a single total. | Leader, Difference, Gap, and Side-by-side | "Who is the clear Leader in sales between the Junior and Senior teams for each region?" |
|  |  | "What is the Difference in price between our product and the competitors, and how large is that Gap across different cities?" |
|  |  | "When we look at Budget vs. Actual spending Side-by-side, which department stayed on track?" |

You must show the Contribution of two different revenue streams (Software and Hardware) to the total regional sales and identify which regional unit has the smallest overall impact.

2. The Dataset
This table shows the revenue (in thousands) divided into two segments. The "Total" is the sum of these segments.

| Regional Business Unit | Software Sales ($k) | Hardware Sales ($k) | Total Revenue ($k) |

| North American Enterprise Division | 450 | 350 | 800 |

| Latin American Growth Sector | 210 | 90 | 300 |
| Middle East and Africa Hub | 140 | 60 | 200 |
